This is arithmetic, not advice. It compounds the numbers you type at a constant rate. Bitcoin does not move at a constant rate, and a projection that assumes it does will be wrong about the path even when it is right about the average. Treat a long-dated CAGR as a scenario, not a forecast — and try a pessimistic one.
